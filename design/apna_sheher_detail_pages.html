📋 About This Job
Shiv Enterprises is looking for experienced Machine Operators for our packaging unit in Chakan, Pune. We run a 3-shift operation producing packaged goods for retail and export.
You will operate automated and semi-automated packaging machines, maintain production targets, and ensure quality. No formal degree required — we value practical experience.
We have worked with Bihar and UP workers for over 8 years and have a strong community culture. Housing is provided in our on-site dormitory (shared 4 persons), free breakfast and lunch, Sunday weekly off. Overtime is paid at 1.5× the hourly rate.

🪔 About This Event
Bihar Samaj Pune invites all members of our community to celebrate Chhath Puja 2025 at Khadakwasla Lake — one of Pune's most beautiful outdoor venues.
Chhath Puja is the most sacred festival for Bihar and Eastern UP communities, celebrated over 4 days. This year includes traditional rituals at sunrise and sunset, prasad distribution, and cultural performances.
Families are welcome. Children's activities will be arranged. Food stalls serving traditional Bihar dishes (litti-chokha, thekua, kaddu ki sabzi) will be set up. No alcohol. Please bring traditional attire. Free parking available at the lake entrance.
