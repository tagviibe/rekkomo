# How to Create Posts in Community

## Quick Guide

### Step 1: Navigate to a Circle
1. Go to **Community** from the main navigation
2. Click on any circle you're a member of (or join one first)
3. You'll see the circle detail page with tabs: Feed | Members | Leaderboard | Apna Gyaan

### Step 2: Create a Post
1. Make sure you're on the **Feed** tab
2. You'll see a post composer box at the top
3. Select the type of post you want to create:
   - **💬 Post** - Regular community post
   - **🚨 SOS** - Emergency help request
   - **📍 Meetup** - Offline meetup proposal
   - **💡 Gyaan Tip** - Share knowledge/tips

### Step 3: Fill in the Details

#### For Regular Post (💬):
- Just type your message in the textarea
- Click "Post" button

#### For SOS (🚨):
- Select a category (Housing, Payment, Medical, Safety, Loan, Legal, Other)
- Choose urgency level (Critical, Urgent, Moderate)
- Describe your situation
- Click "Post" - This will notify ALL circle members immediately

#### For Meetup (📍):
- Enter the location
- Select date & time
- Describe the meetup
- Click "Post" - Members can RSVP

#### For Gyaan Tip (💡):
- Enter a title for your tip
- Select category (Housing, Legal, Health, Work, Government)
- Share your knowledge/tips
- Click "Post" - Community can upvote helpful tips

### Step 4: View Your Post
- Your post will appear in the feed immediately
- SOS posts appear at the top (highest priority)
- Other posts appear chronologically

## Important Notes

1. **You must be a member** of the circle to create posts
2. **SOS posts** send notifications to all circle members
3. **Meetup posts** allow members to RSVP
4. **Gyaan tips** can be upvoted by the community
5. All posts are visible to circle members only

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't see post composer?** Make sure you're a member of the circle
- **Post button disabled?** Fill in all required fields for your post type
- **Error creating post?** Check browser console for details
